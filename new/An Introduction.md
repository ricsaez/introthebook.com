When Paul Erdos came across an elegant solution to a problem, he said it came straight from The Book, a celestial book where God kept the perfect solution to every mathematical problem.

While Erdos’s Book was about mathematics, we could imagine it containing all derivable knowledge about our universe.

Either way, The Book has a prerequisite: a language in which to express its ideas.

Gottfried Leibniz’s dream was to build a Characteristica Universalis: a set of ideas, primitive building blocks, through which any idea could be expressed. 

The Book: An Introduction is a tentative introduction to The Book: its Characteristica Universalis.

None of the ideas presented are mine. They are the result of centuries of work by some of humanity’s greatest minds.

I hope you enjoy discovering these ideas as much as I have.
